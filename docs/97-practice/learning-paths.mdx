<TLDR>
Three reading orders, one for each goal: a manual tester moving into automation, an API tester, and a performance
engineer. Each path lists pages in the order to read them. The hour estimates are my rough guesses, not measured study times.
</TLDR>

## What and why

The handbook has many pages, and it can feel like a wall. A learning path tells you which pages matter for your goal
and in what order. You do not need to read everything. Paths share some pages, so you can switch goals later without
losing work.

## How to use a path

Read each page in order. After the test-design pages, try the [Exercises: fundamentals and design](/practice/exercises-fundamentals-and-design).
After the automation and layer pages, try the [Exercises: automation and layers](/practice/exercises-automation-and-layers).
Run the TaskFlow app from `examples/taskflow/` when a page asks you to try a rule. Keep a short list of the answers you
got wrong. That list tells you more than the hour count does.

:::note[Time estimates]
The hours below are my estimates of reading time plus exercises. They are not measured. Your time will differ,
especially if you already know some of the topics.
:::
